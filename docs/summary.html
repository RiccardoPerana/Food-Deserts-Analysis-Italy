Reading these figures

  • A settlement is a town or village as ISTAT records it (a centro abitato), so a comune's outlying villages are measured separately from its main town. Hamlets and scattered houses are not analysed.
  • Shops come from OpenStreetMap. Where ISTAT's business register counts more supermarkets and grocers in a comune than OpenStreetMap maps, the missing ones are placed in its main town or largest villages, and treated like any other shop.
  • Distances are real walking routes, not straight lines. Residents aged 65+ are estimated from each comune's share, since ISTAT does not publish ages per settlement.
  • Full method and limitations: the project README.
